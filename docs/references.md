# Reading list: data quality & reproducibility

A short, curated reading list on producing trustworthy, reproducible data
analyses. Every DOI below was checked to resolve.

1. Wilson G, Bryan J, Cranston K, Kitzes J, Nederbragt L, Teal TK (2017).
   *Good enough practices in scientific computing.* PLoS Computational Biology
   13(6): e1005510. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005510

2. Sandve GK, Nekrutenko A, Taylor J, Hovig E (2013).
   *Ten simple rules for reproducible computational research.* PLoS
   Computational Biology 9(10): e1003285.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003285

3. Wilkinson MD, Dumontier M, Aalbersberg IJ, et al. (2016).
   *The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and
   stewardship.* Scientific Data 3: 160018.
   https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18

4. Peng RD (2011). *Reproducible Research in Computational Science.* Science
   334(6060): 1226–1227. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1213847

5. Wilson G, Aruliah DA, Brown CT, et al. (2014). *Best Practices for
   Scientific Computing.* PLoS Biology 12(1): e1001745.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001745
